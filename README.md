# HUDHUD

**هُدْهُد** لعبة تعليمية إسلامية ثلاثية اللغات للأطفال من 8 إلى 10 سنوات، مبنية كتجربة 2.5D غنية بالرسوم والتحريك.

**HUDHUD** is a trilingual 2.5D Islamic educational game for Muslim children aged 8–10.

## Current product decisions

- One age group: 8–10 years.
- One progressive gameplay path; no age-track selector.
- German is the primary player-facing language. Arabic is the editorial source language, with English also supported.
- Nine content units.
- One complete pilot stage per unit, with eight steps per stage: 72 steps in the first design batch.
- Web and PWA first.
- Visual-first design with short sound effects, no songs.
- Final visual identity: a glowing five-feather hoopoe crest beside the rounded Latin wordmark HUDHUD.
- Tagline: **Learn your faith while you play**
- The main key art uses a bright mosque courtyard with the two playable children, the final logo, and separate DE / EN / AR language controls.
- Original 2.5D assets; Pharma-Coach may inform programming ideas only.
